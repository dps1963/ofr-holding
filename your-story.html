Your Story

Your life story, in your own voice — made into a book to keep.

Your Story helps you tell the story of your life, one memory at a time. Talk, answer gentle prompts and questions, add photos, and arrange it all into chapters. When you are ready, turn it into a digital book or a printed book to keep and to pass on.

Why we made Your Story

My daughter gave me a fill-in journal, and with it one simple request: Tell me your story, Dad.

I was touched, and I meant to do it. Then I opened it and found page after page of blank lines. Sitting down to write felt daunting. Where do you start? What is worth putting down? I put the journal down, and somehow never picked it up again.

Yet talking has always come easily to me. Ask me a question and the words come. Talking was never the problem. Writing was.

That is the whole idea behind Your Story. You answer a question by speaking, as if the person asking were sitting across the table. Your spoken words are turned into text you can edit. You tidy them, add a photo or two, and chapter by chapter a book takes shape: one your family can hold, read and keep.

— Dave, founder, Bring Me Sunshine Group

How it works

Three gentle steps, at your own pace. Start with a single memory, stop whenever you like, and come back when you are ready.

  1. Record

    Just talk. Answer questions and prompts by speaking or typing. Your recordings are written down for you, and you can add photos and short videos.

  2. Write and arrange

    Edit the words, choose what goes in, and arrange your memories into chapters, with your photos alongside.

  3. Make your book

    Preview your finished book, then buy it as a digital book (PDF) or have it printed and posted to you.
