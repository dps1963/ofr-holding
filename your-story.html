Your Story

Your life story, in your own words — made into a book.

Your Story helps you record and write the story of your life. Talk, answer gentle prompts and questions, add photos, and arrange it all into chapters. When you are ready, turn it into a digital book or a printed book to keep and to pass on.

Your Story is a service of BMSG (Bring Me Sunshine Group), the operator of OurFamilyRocks.com.

How it works

Three steps, at your own pace. You can stop and come back whenever you like.

  1. Record

    Answer questions and prompts by speaking or typing. Your recordings are written down for you, and you can add photos and short videos.

  2. Write and arrange

    Edit the words, choose what goes in, and arrange your memories into chapters, with your photos alongside.

  3. Make your book

    Preview your finished book, then buy it as a digital book (PDF) or have it printed and posted to you.

How your purchase is delivered

Digital book

As soon as your payment is confirmed, your PDF is made and a download link appears in your Your Story account. You can download it again later from the same place.

Printed book

Your book is printed and posted to the address you give at checkout. Shipping is charged per order, with one flat rate for New Zealand addresses and a higher flat rate for overseas addresses (currently NZ$15 within New Zealand and NZ$30 overseas). The amount is shown at checkout before you pay.

The expected delivery time is shown at checkout.

Gift

Once your payment is confirmed, the gift email is sent to the person you chose (or on the date you picked). The link in the email works for 60 days. You can see whether it has been opened and accepted from your Gifts list.
