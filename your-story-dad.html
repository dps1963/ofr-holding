Every family has one voice they will wish they had kept. Your Story Dad is a quiet place for Dad to tell his — one small story at a time — for the only audience that matters: his own family.

▾

The moment we're building for

Every dad says “one day I'll write it all down.”

The stories are all there — how he met Mum, the job that went sideways, the town he grew up in, the things he'd do all over again. They come out beside a fire, on a long drive, at the end of a birthday dinner. And then everyone goes home, and they're gone.

Your Story Dad catches them — in his own words, in his own voice — before “one day” runs out of days.

How it will work

Four small steps, no homework.

1

Just catch the thought

When a memory visits — on a walk, in the car, by the fire — Dad says it out loud or types a few lines. No title, no date, no pressure. The thought is kept the moment it's caught.

2

Thoughts grow into chapters

Little recordings and notes gather into chapters — childhood, first jobs, us kids — that Dad can retell, reorder and polish whenever he likes. It becomes his book.

3

Dad chooses the circle

The book is private from the first word. Dad picks exactly who may open it — his children, and whoever else he names. Nobody is in the room unless he invited them.

4

The stories arrive home

When a chapter is ready, the family hears it in Dad's own voice — whether the kids are in the next room or on the other side of the world.

And it stays in the family

His words are for his people, not for a feed.

This is the opposite of social media. There is no public page, no likes, no strangers, no ads.

  • Private by default — every story starts visible to Dad alone.
  • Family-only audience — each listener is someone Dad chose by name.
  • Nothing leaves the family unless Dad himself says so.

From a fireside in New Zealand to a kitchen table in Scotland — a story told once is a story kept for good.

Distance is exactly why we're building this.
