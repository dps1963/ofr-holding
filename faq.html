The basics

What is Our Family Rocks?
Our Family Rocks is a family app that brings your plans, chores, challenges and memories together in one calm, shared space — so weekends run smoother, kids stay motivated, and the good moments don't slip by.
What does it cost?
Our Family Rocks is free to download on the App Store and Google Play, with one shared family account.
Which devices does it work on?
iPhone, iPad and Android. One shared family account keeps everyone in the loop across every family member's device, with instant sync.
Does it work offline?
Yes. It works offline on adventures and syncs automatically when you're back online.

What it does

What are the four tools?
Plan (a shared family calendar), Chores & challenges (turn tidy-up into a game), Explore (adventure ideas), and Keep (a private family memory stream). Four simple tools that work together — designed for grown-ups to run and kids to love.
How does the shared calendar work?
Outings, sport, birthdays and appointments live in one view, with reminders that reach every member — not just one parent.
What are pebble points?
Kids earn pebble points for finished chores and family challenges, redeemable for rewards you set. It turns tidy-up time into a game.
What is the private memory stream?
Keep is a family-only album and journal. Capture a moment, add a line, and build your family's story. It's never shared publicly.
What are 'adventure ideas'?
Explore gives fresh, age-appropriate suggestions for weekends, rainy days and school holidays — nearby or right at the kitchen table.

Getting started & who can join

How do I set it up?
Three steps: build your family space and add each person as their own 'drop' (parents, kids, grandparents too); add your first plans, chores or a challenge; then tick things off, earn points and save photos and stories to your private memory stream. It takes minutes.
Can grandparents and extended family join?
Yes — add parents, kids and grandparents, each as their own drop, so the whole family stays in the loop.
How do I download it?
Download Our Family Rocks free on the App Store or Google Play, add your drops, and start your first family adventure.

Privacy & safety

Is it private and safe for children?
Yes. Profiles are simple and safe, designed for young children, and everything is private by default — nothing is ever shared publicly.
How is our family's data handled?
Your family's plans and memories are private to your family account and stored on EU-resident infrastructure under GDPR. We never use Google Analytics or a Meta Pixel, and your memory stream is never public.
