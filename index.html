What it does

Everything a family juggles, in one app

Four simple tools that work together, designed for grown-ups to run and kids to love.

Plan

Shared family calendar

Outings, sport, birthdays and appointments in one view, with reminders that reach every member — not just one parent.

Do

Chores & challenges

Turn tidy-up time into a game. Kids earn pebble points for finished chores and family challenges, redeemable for rewards you set.

Explore

Adventure ideas

Fresh, age-appropriate suggestions for weekends, rainy days and school holidays — nearby or at the kitchen table.

Keep

Private memory stream

A family-only album and journal. Capture the moment, add a line, and build your family's story — never shared publicly.

Getting started

Set up in minutes

Step one

Build your family

Create your family space and add each person as their own drop — parents, kids, grandparents too.

Step two

Add your first plans

Put the week on the shared calendar, assign a few chores, or start a challenge. Everyone sees who's doing what.

Step three

Collect the moments

Tick things off, earn points, and save the photos and stories to your private memory stream.

The whole family, in your pocket

  • Simple, safe profiles designed for young children
  • Private by default — nothing is ever shared publicly
  • Instant sync across every family member's device
  • Works offline on adventures, syncs when you're back
